Guide your rocket swarm through multiplier gates, dodge stone traps and crush the enemy fortress.

HOW TO PLAY
• Drag in the battlefield to aim your launcher.
• Fire rockets through glowing × gates to multiply the swarm.
• Stone walls destroy rockets on contact.
• Every surviving rocket damages the enemy fortress.
• Siege Blast clears obstacles once charged.
• Campaign gets harder every level. Endless continues until your castle falls.
