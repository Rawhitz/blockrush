🔥 Bengali Jora Guti rules
• If a player is still trapped in base, a 6 is guaranteed by the 5th roll.
• Two same-colour pawns meeting on a ⭐ star form a Jora.
• Jora moves only on even rolls: 6→3, 4→2, 2→1.
• Nobody may overtake a Jora. A single pawn cannot kill one.
• Only another Jora can kill a Jora.
• A Jora can split only while standing on a ⭐ star.
• After one full circuit, pawns enter their own coloured home lane and can never return to the outer track.
